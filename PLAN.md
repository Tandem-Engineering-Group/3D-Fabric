## How it feeds the business goals

- **Prove the product** — render → prototype → batch → paying customers by week 10.
- **Fund it without debt** — monthly Amber entries + one serious Motor City Match swing.
- **Build the audience** — book, bags, and launch tell one Detroit story; each feeds the others.
- **Keep the machine cheap** — the pipeline specs a new design in ~17 s; money goes to fabric, not drafting.

**Flywheel:** book builds the story → story sells purses and strengthens grant apps →
grants fund production → production gives the book its material.
